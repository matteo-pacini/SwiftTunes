## 0.1.0

The first release.

- Endless music by genre, composed on your Mac by ACE-Step 1.5 Turbo. Nothing
  is sent anywhere once the model is downloaded.
- Nine genres to start with. Add, rename or delete genres, and set each one's
  prompt, variations, tempo range, colours and Singing switch.
- Turn the composer on and it writes new tunes and keeps two ready ahead. The
  newest 50 tunes of a genre are kept.
- The This round list shows every tune of the round in one order, keeps the
  playing tune in view, and lets you play any of them again. Shuffle or play
  in the order made; repeat all, one or off.
- Previous goes back a tune within the first three seconds, and otherwise
  starts the tune again.
- Sung genres have words written by the model, shown under the playing tune.
  You can also ask for one tune sung with words you write.
- Scrap a tune and bring it back within ten seconds.
- Keep a tune for good, so the newest-50 rule never removes it.
- Drag a tune out, share it, copy its path, show it in Finder or export a
  copy.
- Click the playing tune's name to rename it.
- A volume slider under the controls sets the app's own volume, not the
  Mac's.
- Remove the 7.57 GiB model from the menu when you want the space back; your
  tunes stay and keep playing.
- Every tune gets a picture drawn from its name, tempo and key.
